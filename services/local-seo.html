Frequently asked questions

What is local GEO (generative engine optimization)?

It’s making sure your business is the answer when someone asks ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity “who does this near me?” It’s driven by accurate structured data, your Google Business Profile, an llms.txt file, and making sure AI crawlers can actually read your site — not by backlinks.

What does the $550 monthly plan actually include?

A typical month: Google Business Profile posts and review replies, a review campaign to that month’s customers, one new location or service page, and a plain-English report showing what people searched, where they came from, and the calls you got. Roughly 3–5 hours of real work with something to show for it. Month-to-month, cancel anytime, and if there’s genuinely nothing useful to do that month, we tell you.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. The Setup is a one-off. The monthly plan is cancel-anytime. We’d rather keep you because the work is worth it than because a contract traps you.

Can you guarantee I’ll be number one on Google?

No, and anyone who promises that isn’t being straight with you. We fix what’s structurally broken, get your Google listing and reviews working, and give you an honest read on what’s left to do.

Do I need the monthly plan?

Only if you’re actively trying to grow — reaching new suburbs, wanting a steady flow of reviews. If you just needed the one-time fix, we’ll say so. We don’t charge a retainer for nothing.
