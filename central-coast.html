Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from Central Coast businesses considering an AI development project.

Are you actually based on the Central Coast?

Yes — we are headquartered on the NSW Central Coast. We’re a local business, not a national agency with a local address. You can meet us in person, and we understand the local business environment because we operate in it.

Is AI development affordable for small local businesses?

It depends on the scope. Our sprint model means you’re not signing a large retainer — you commit to one sprint at a time, each with a fixed price and defined deliverables. A single well-scoped AI feature can be delivered for a fraction of what a full development agency would charge for the same result.

Do I need a technical background to work with you?

No. We work from a two-paragraph brief. You describe the problem and what success looks like — we handle the technical architecture, the LLM integration, and the infrastructure. Most of our clients are founders or business owners, not engineers.

Can you help if I have an existing website or tool I want to improve?

Yes — that’s what our automation retrofit service is for. We add AI to what you already have without rebuilding it. One new feature, measured against a success metric, delivered in 2–6 weeks.

How do I get started?

Use the contact form. Two paragraphs on the problem and what success looks like is all we need. We reply within one business day with a realistic scope. No pitch call required to get a straight answer.
