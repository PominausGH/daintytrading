Frequently Asked Questions — Australian AI Development

Common questions from Australian businesses considering an AI development engagement.

Is Dainty Trading based in Australia?

Yes. We are an Australia-based studio, operating in AEST. We work async with Australian and international clients and can do real-time calls during business hours across all Australian timezones.

What currencies do you invoice in?

Yes — AUD for Australian clients, USD for international clients. Simple and flexible.

Do you understand Australian data privacy requirements?

Yes. We build with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) as a default architecture consideration — not an afterthought. For products handling personal information, we can route LLM calls through AU-region cloud infrastructure and help you build a compliant data processing architecture.

What Australian industries have you built AI for?

Education, service businesses, content marketing, personal finance, field operations, and health and wellness. Our portfolio spans these sectors — all live products, not case studies.

Do you work with Australian startups or only established businesses?

Primarily founders and product teams at startups and scale-ups. Our sprint-based model suits teams that need fast, measurable results without large agency overheads or multi-month contracts.

How quickly can you start?

We scope new projects within 48 hours of receiving a brief. If we’re a fit, the first sprint can usually start within a week. We take a limited number of client engagements per quarter to ensure quality.
