What happens next

  • Within one business day — you’ll get a written reply with our read on the project and any clarifying questions.
  • Within a week — if it’s a fit, we send a one-page scope for the first sprint with a fixed price and a hard end date.
  • Within two weeks — first working slice, deployed somewhere you can use it.
  • From week three — we iterate with you: weekly demos, one feature per sprint, revising based on what you and real users see, until it’s a product you can rely on. Typically paying customers by week eight, or we’ll tell you why not.
