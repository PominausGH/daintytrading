What Sydney Businesses Are Building With AI

The highest-ROI AI use cases we’re seeing across Sydney’s industries right now.

01

Professional services automation

Sydney law firms, accountancies, and consultancies are automating document review, client intake, and report drafting. The bottleneck is always the same: skilled people spending time on repetitive language tasks. We remove that bottleneck without removing the human review step.

02

SaaS AI feature retrofits

Sydney has a strong B2B SaaS ecosystem. Companies with existing products are adding AI-powered features — draft generation, smart triage, structured extraction — to retain customers and close deals against AI-native competitors. We bolt these in without rewriting the product.

03

Trades, real estate, and hospitality businesses across Greater Sydney are losing leads to voicemail and slow response times. Everyring.ai — our missed-call follow-up tool — turns a missed call into an AI-drafted, personalised follow-up in under 60 seconds.

AI-native product development

Sydney founders with ideas for AI-first products — tools that use LLMs as their core feature, not a bolt-on — need a team that understands both the product and the AI layer. We’ve shipped 35 of these. We know the failure modes before you hit them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from Sydney businesses considering an AI development engagement.

Are you based in Sydney?

We are based on the NSW Central Coast — about 90 minutes from Sydney CBD. We work with Sydney clients remotely and can meet in-person for kick-offs or reviews. All project work is delivered async with daily written updates so location rarely matters for day-to-day collaboration.

What industries do you work with in Sydney?

Professional services, SaaS and tech companies, service businesses (trades, real estate, hospitality), and fintech. We’ve built across all of these — the AI approaches are often similar even when the industries aren’t.

How quickly can we get started?

Send us a two-paragraph brief via the contact form. We reply within one business day with a realistic scope — no discovery call required to get a straight answer. If we’re a fit, the first sprint can start within a week.

Do you do fixed-price work or time and materials?

Fixed scope, fixed price per sprint. Each sprint has defined deliverables agreed upfront. We don’t quietly add hours — if scope changes, we rescope with you before we proceed.

Can you handle Australian data privacy requirements?

Yes. We build with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 as a default architecture consideration. For products handling personal information, we route LLM calls through AU-region infrastructure and architect data flows to minimise cross-border transfers.
