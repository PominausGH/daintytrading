Trade Price Shutters

The same client behind Shuttersmith — after our SEO/GEO work sent them more business than their first shutters company could hold. So they backed a second one: Trade Price Shutters, a trade-price, self-install spinoff. We built that site too.

Client  Trade Price Shutters Stack  Next.js Category  Client · Home improvement · E-commerce Status  In staging, launching soon

The problem

This is the second half of the Shuttersmith story. Our SEO/GEO work on their first business worked well enough that the client decided to launch a second, complementary one: plantation shutters sold at trade price, with a self-install option for customers who’d rather measure and fit their own than pay for a full install. That’s a genuinely different product to sell online — it needed real quoting tools, not another brochure site.

What we built

A full Next.js site built around a measure-it-yourself flow: customers can use an on-site calculator to size their own windows, then choose between a professional install or a DIY self-install kit priced roughly 20% below the installed rate. Pricing is transparent and flat — the client’s call, and one we built the calculator around rather than around a sales team.

SEO/GEO was built in from the start, the same way it was for Shuttersmith and Edwards Kirby: full metadata, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, an llms.txt for AI-search visibility, and structured data — so a brand-new business isn’t starting from zero on search the way most launches do.

Where we are

The build is complete and in staging, finishing final pricing sign-off ahead of going live at tradepriceshutters.com.au.
