The timezone actually works

Australia is 9–10 hours ahead of the UK. That means our day ends while yours is just starting — you receive a complete written update on everything built before you sit down at your desk. There’s also a brief late-afternoon window in the UK (around 4–6pm GMT) that overlaps with early morning here, useful for a quick call when needed. Most clients say it’s one of the smoothest async arrangements they’ve had.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from UK companies considering working with an Australian AI studio.

How does the 9–10 hour timezone gap work in practice?

Better than you might expect. Our working day ends as yours begins, so you receive a full written update on everything completed before your morning coffee. There’s also a genuine overlap window — around 4–6pm UK time maps to 1–3am our time, but our early morning (6–8am AEST) lines up with late afternoon in the UK. Most clients use this for a quick weekly call if needed, and handle everything else async. Within one sprint, the rhythm feels natural.

What do you charge compared to UK agencies?

We don’t publish rates — scope drives price too much for fixed-price projects. What we can say: Australian market rates billed in GBP are meaningfully lower than equivalent London agency rates for the same quality work. Send us a brief and we’ll give you a specific number within 48 hours.

Do you work within UK-style contracts and NDAs?

Yes. We sign NDAs before detailed conversations if required. Our client agreements are written in plain English and can accommodate standard UK contract frameworks. We’ve worked with clients who have their own MSAs and can operate within those.

What if I want to start small?

That’s exactly how we structure it. Sprint one is deliberately scoped small — the smallest thing that proves the core idea works. You see something real in 2 weeks at a low cost relative to the total project. If it’s not right, you’ve spent very little to find out. If it is, we scope sprint two together.

Who owns the code?

You do. Full source code, infrastructure configuration, and deployed product are handed over at the end of each sprint. We retain no rights to what we build for you.
