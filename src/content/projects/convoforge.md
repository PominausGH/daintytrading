<h2>The problem</h2>
<p>Communication coaching has always been a luxury good — expensive, infrequent, and impossible to apply in the moment when it matters. We wanted a coach that learns your specific patterns, gives you targeted micro-drills between meetings, and rides along during the meetings themselves with the lightest possible touch.</p>

<h2>What we built</h2>
<p>ConvoForge has four pillars. <strong>Microlearning</strong> ships a 60–90 second drill every weekday, calibrated to your weakest signal. <strong>Speech analysis</strong> ingests recorded calls and rates filler-word density, pace, energy, and turn-taking. <strong>Body-language analysis</strong> uses webcam ML to flag posture and gaze patterns. <strong>Avatar roleplay</strong> lets you rehearse a hard conversation with a configurable AI counterpart.</p>
<p>The live meeting overlay is the piece we’re proudest of: a small, opt-in heads-up display during a Zoom or Google Meet that shows pace, monologue length, and a single one-word nudge when something obvious is happening. No transcripts, no advice, no AI voice in your ear — just one number you actually look at.</p>

<h2>The AI angle</h2>
<p>Multimodal end-to-end. Audio runs through transcription and prosody analysis. Video runs through pose and gaze detection. The combined signal is structured into a per-session report that Claude turns into a narrative summary and, more importantly, a single actionable drill for tomorrow. The overlay uses small, fast models behind a strict latency budget so the loop closes in real time.</p>

<h2>How it’s used</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Founders running fundraises</strong> who need to compress weeks of pitch reps into days.</li>
<li><strong>Sales teams</strong> looking for measurable improvement on call structure and energy.</li>
<li><strong>Anyone preparing for a hard conversation</strong> — a difficult 1:1, a board update, a negotiation — who wants to rehearse against a counterpart that doesn’t flinch.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What it taught us</h2>
<p>Behavioural change happens at the smallest unit. The temptation with multimodal AI is to throw the whole report at the user. We learned to suppress 95% of it — one drill, one number, one nudge. The compound effect over weeks is bigger than any single 40-page session report ever produced.</p>
