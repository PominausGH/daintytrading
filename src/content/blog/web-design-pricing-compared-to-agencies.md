<p>We <a href="/blog/website-design-cost-australia-vs-offshore.html">wrote last week</a> about offshore vs. Australian pricing. The question we got back most was simpler: forget offshore, how do we compare to other local agencies? Roughly like this — a freelancer is $1,500–$5,000 for the build, nothing else. A boutique agency doing custom work is $3,000–$10,000+ for the build, then SEO gets quoted separately, usually as an $800–$2,500/month retainer if you want it at all. A branding-led agency — the ones who pitch you on "digital strategy" — starts around $15,000 and still treats SEO as an add-on line item. We're $6,900 AUD for a custom build, or $4,900 if you commit to 12 months of Growth after, and Local SEO & GEO setup is in that number, not quoted after.</p>

<h2>Why we don't publish a table naming who charges what</h2>
<p>Because it'd be wrong within a month — agency pricing moves constantly and none of it is published anywhere reliable to begin with, so any table is really just us guessing at competitors' rate cards and putting a specific dollar figure next to someone else's name. The bands above are what we see quoted back to us by prospects who came from a competitor first, which is a more honest source than us inventing numbers for a table. If you've got an actual quote in hand, send it to us — we'll tell you straight whether ours is actually better value for what you need, including if it isn't.</p>

<h2>The bundling is the actual difference, not the headline number</h2>
<p>A $4,000 build quote and a $6,900 build quote look like the same decision — pick the cheaper one — until you notice one of them has no SEO in it at all. The industry norm is to sell the build, then sell SEO as a second, ongoing product, because that's a better business model for the agency: one project fee plus a recurring retainer beats one project fee alone. We priced it as one thing because for a business that doesn't already have an in-house marketing function, a website with no <a href="/blog/what-is-geo-generative-engine-optimization.html">GEO</a> or <a href="/blog/local-seo-vs-seo-vs-geo-difference.html">local SEO</a> foundation isn't a smaller version of the finished product — it's a different, mostly useless product that happens to look the same in a screenshot.</p>
